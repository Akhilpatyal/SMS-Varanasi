Spark Performing Arts Cultural Club

Diversity is the feature of India. At SMS we try to portray such diversity in a Healthy manner so that it may help in better exposure to students and overall personality development of students. We believe that education thrives when the views, cultures, and values of the entire community are fully engaged. Spark Club offer students a Place for learning and understanding unity lying with in the diversity, so that they can be successful managers in their upcoming career. The centers are open to all members of the SMS Varanasi.

With The objective to promote culture and performing art related activities following activities have been among regular course of action by the students of SMS:

Dance: Solo and Group

Singing: Solo & Group

Drama

Fashion shows

Celebrating Days of importance

Mimicry
