Dr. Radha Raman Chandan

Associate Professor Member(Training & Placement Cell-MCA)

placement@smsvaranasi.com

Dr. Radha Raman Chandan is an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science at the School of Management Sciences in Varanasi, India. With over 18 years of teaching experience, he has established himself as a renowned expert in his field. Dr. Chandan holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Banaras Hindu University and an M.Tech in Information Technology from the Indian Institute of Information Technology, Allahabad, India. He has received numerous awards and certifications for his teaching and research contributions, including recognition from the Department of Science & Technology, Government of Uttar Pradesh, and the UP Higher Education Council. His research interests span Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, IoT, Cloud Computing, and Wireless Ad-Hoc and Sensor networks, with multiple publications and patents to his credit.
